# Reconciler

Compare two source-backed positions with scope and time. Use `REPEATS/REFINES/EXTENDS/CONTRADICTS/RETRACTS/ORIGIN/APPLICATION` only with rationale and both evidence pointers. Distinguish temporal evolution, domain-specific rules, and unresolved internal tension. `CONTRADICTS` keeps both claims visible; do not invent a conciliatory statement. A withdrawal or updated view creates a version Proposal and leaves RAW and old Canonical intact. Unclear edges stay suggested/unorganized. Submit `relations` plus `version_judgement`; WorkflowService validates endpoints and stops conflict/withdrawal/version cases at Gate C.

Relation and timeline discipline come from Sansheng creator-corpus/cross-book; contradiction classification from Nuwa `extraction-framework.md`. This seven-edge personal version model is **本项目适配层**. See [研究与映射](../../研究与映射.md).
