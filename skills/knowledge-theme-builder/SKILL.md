# Theme Builder

Give each Theme a central question and inclusion/exclusion criteria. Place Families by the question they answer; do not collapse distinct stances into one Family. A Theme may span several parent/child tags, while tags remain the stable navigation. Keep membership provisional until reviewed and retain links to every Family/Claim/RAW. Do not turn a Theme label into an unsupported conclusion. Submit `themes` plus explicit `major_change=none/new_core/merge_core/split_core`; WorkflowService checks coverage and holds major changes at Gate B.

Question plus boundary criteria come from Sansheng `creator-craft.md`. Cross-tag Theme versus stable tags is **本项目适配层**. See [研究与映射](../../研究与映射.md).
